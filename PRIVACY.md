# Privacy Policy for Ambient Light for Bilibili

Last updated: September 29, 2026

**Ambient Light for Bilibili** is an open-source browser extension designed to provide hardware-accelerated ambient lighting effects for Bilibili video players.

### 1. Data Collection and Usage
Ambient Light for Bilibili **does not collect, store, transmit, or sell any personal data or personally identifiable information (PII)**. All operations and video frame rendering occur entirely locally within your browser client.

### 2. Permissions Explained
The extension requests only minimal permissions strictly required to deliver its core functionality:
- **`storage`**: Used exclusively to store your personal user preferences (such as light spread, blur radius, brightness, saturation, and enable/disable toggle) locally in your browser.
- **`host_permissions` (`*://*.bilibili.com/*`)**: Used exclusively to detect Bilibili video player elements and synchronize ambient light canvas rendering on video playback pages. No webpage content, network requests, browsing history, or cookies are collected or shared.

### 3. Third-Party Services
This extension does not integrate with any third-party analytics, tracking services, advertising networks, or remote telemetry servers.

### 4. Open Source
The full source code of this extension is publicly auditable and hosted on GitHub:
https://github.com/vainreef/ambient-light-for-bilibili

### 5. Contact
If you have any questions regarding this privacy policy, you can open an issue on the GitHub repository or contact the developer directly.
